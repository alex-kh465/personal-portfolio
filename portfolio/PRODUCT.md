# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters hiring for AI and Data Engineering roles. They are looking for technical competence, clear communication, and an organized approach to complex problems.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio to showcase projects, experience, and research. It exists to secure interviews and demonstrate proficiency in AI, Data Engineering, and modern web development.

## Brand Personality
Technical, precise, and capable. It should feel like the workspace of an expert engineer—focused on data and function without sacrificing aesthetic quality.

## Anti-references
"AI slop". Avoid generic 2026 AI tropes: no overly rounded cards (32px+), no meaningless gradient text over gradient backgrounds, no "ghost-card" (border + heavy drop shadow) components, no arbitrary diagonal stripes, and no repetitive "01 ABOUT" eyebrow kickers above every section. The design must be deliberate and restrained.

## Design Principles
- **Practice what you preach**: The interface should be as clean, performant, and precise as the data pipelines being showcased.
- **Show, don't tell**: Let the work and metrics speak for themselves with high-signal, low-noise layouts.
- **Expert confidence**: Restraint in motion and color. Use them purposefully to highlight key data or interactions, not as mere decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain high contrast (≥4.5:1 for body text) to ensure readability. Provide reduced motion alternatives for any complex canvas or layout animations.
